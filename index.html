01 / About

Why this lab exists

Good AI research should be possible beyond the largest models and the highest-resource languages.

CLiP Lab brings together questions in language, vision, and efficient machine learning. Bengali and other languages with limited training resources deserve better tools and more careful evaluation. We use focused experiments to understand what models learn, where they struggle, and which design choices matter when data and compute are limited.

We are a student-led research initiative associated with RUET, Bangladesh.

07 / Approach

How we approach research

Ask a focused question. Run a controlled experiment. Report what happened, including the limits.

We value honest evaluation and reproducible work. A useful result explains why a model succeeds, where it fails, and which conditions change its behavior. Small, carefully designed experiments often make those patterns easier to see.
