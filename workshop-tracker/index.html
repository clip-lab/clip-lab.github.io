BLPTASK 03

BLP Datathon

Collect and hand-transcribe regional Bangla speech to expose dialect failures in ASR.

Bangla Speech Data
17 Jan 2027Confirmed+
In simple terms

Volunteer teams record natural speech from Bangla dialect speakers, manually transcribe it, and submit the audio plus metadata. Organizers test open-source speech recognizers on the accepted clips. Unlike typical ML leaderboards, this is a community data-collection and analysis challenge.

System deadline
17 Jan 2027
Paper / report
15 Feb 2027
Earlier action
15 Dec 2026 - registration
Tasks & tracks
Field recordings
Collect natural, consented conversations from adult speakers of different Bangla dialects, around 10 minutes per session.
Human transcripts
Manually type exactly what was said, preserving dialect words rather than normalizing to standard Bangla.
ASR stress test
Organizers measure which accents and dialects cause recognition errors. Teams receive quality/diversity-based points.
Evaluation

Accepted contribution quality, dialect diversity and ASR difficulty; team/member points and leaderboard.

Practical participation

Free registration; ages 18+; teams of 1-5. Human transcription only: do not use automatic speech recognition, chatbots, or edited/AI-generated voices. Deadline 23:59 Bangladesh time (UTC+6).

Agents Safety Classification

SemEvalTASK 07

CLaS - Cross-Lingual Steering

Change an LLM output language by intervening inside its activations, not merely prompting.

Multilingual Interpretability LLMs
31 Jan 2027Confirmed+
In simple terms

Given a question in one language, steer a frozen/selected LLM to answer in a different target language while staying relevant. A prompt saying "answer in X" is NOT a valid official steering method: you must change internal computations at inference time.

System deadline
31 Jan 2027
Paper / report
Feb 2027, tentative
Earlier action
31 Dec 2026 - register
Tasks & tracks
T1 - High-resource steering
90 language directions among Arabic, German, English, Spanish, French, Japanese, Chinese, Russian, Hindi and Korean.
T2 - Lower-resource targets
100 directions from those ten source languages into Czech, Greek, Persian, Vietnamese, Ukrainian, Maltese, Norwegian, Swahili, Thai and Turkish.
T3 - Full multilingual steering
Steer from the other 19 languages into Japanese, Russian or German; evaluated per target and per model.
Bonus - Multilingual refusal
Produce appropriate refusals in a requested language; detailed protocol forthcoming.
Evaluation

Target-language compliance AND response relevance (separate official rankings by model/track).

Practical participation

Required evaluated models: Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct and tiny-aya-global. Interventions may involve neurons, attention heads, activation vectors, subspaces, SAEs or probes. Organizer executes code on its GPUs. Two test runs plus one final run per track.

Task milestones
  1. 31 Dec 2026Registration deadline
  2. 10 Jan 2027Evaluation and system submissions open
  3. 31 Jan 2027Final system submissions close
!

If you care about representation-level multilingual alignment, this task is especially directly relevant.

Retrieval Reasoning RAG
TBA; no later than 31 JanLatest possible+
In simple terms

Most retrievers match similar words. RETECO tests whether your retriever can identify the right time period, resolve context from earlier conversation turns and provide evidence for answers.

Rank documents for a time-dependent question that may require information from multiple periods.

SemEvalTASK 03

Semantic Change Across Time

Discover word senses and measure how their prevalence changes over ten historical time periods.

Multilingual Lexical semantics Clustering
By 31 Jan 2027Latest possible+
In simple terms

A word such as "mouse" changes as new senses appear and spread. Given dated uses across ten time windows, identify the meanings and quantify how the sense distribution moves over time.

System deadline
By 31 Jan 2027
Paper / report
Feb 2027, tentative
Earlier action
10 Jan 2027 - evaluation
Tasks & tracks
1a - Diachronic sense assignment
Group word occurrences into meanings without a predefined sense inventory; assessed with BCubed F1.
1b - Sense dynamics
Estimate change/sense-shift scores through time; compare rankings with Spearman correlation.

For a given candidate sense, classify each use as expressing it or not; evaluated with average F1.
Evaluation

BCubed F1; Spearman rank correlation; and per-word averaged binary F1.

Practical participation

Six languages: Swedish, English, Italian, Spanish, Dutch and Russian. Historical contexts span about 1880 to 2023. CodaBench for participation.

Time zones and confidence

AoE: 23:59 in UTC-12; in Dhaka this falls at 17:59 the following calendar day. BLP papers and BanglaKnight explicitly use AoE. The BLP datathon instead uses 23:59 Bangladesh time. SemEval-wide dates do not specify a universal exact hour here.

Confirmed means specified by the task; latest possible means a central upper bound; tentative means the organizer may revise it. Free task entry never automatically guarantees free in-person conference attendance or an accepted paper.
